## SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM (5 minutes)

*[Click to Problem slide / keep QFlow logo on screen]*

"Before I show you the platform, let me be honest about what we're solving.

QCI operates across **multiple Divisions**, each Division has **multiple Verticals**, each Vertical runs **multiple Projects**, each Project has **Milestones**, and each Milestone is broken into **Tasks**. That's five levels of hierarchy.

Today, managing this looks like:

- **Emails** for approvals — buried in inboxes, no audit trail
- **Excel for budgets** — one person's version, nobody else's reality
- **WhatsApp for escalations** — nothing is documented
- **Meetings to find status** — instead of meetings to make decisions
- **No single view** for an executive who needs to see the health of all 20 projects at once

The result? Projects overrun. Budgets are guessed. Accountability is unclear. And when an auditor asks 'who approved what and when?' — we scramble.

Q-Flow doesn't just digitize these processes. It makes them **transparent, traceable, and fast.**"

## SECTION 3 — PLATFORM OVERVIEW (5 minutes)

*[Open Q-Flow on the main screen. Show the dashboard.]*

"Before I go deep into each feature, let me give you the 30,000-foot view.

Q-Flow is built around **three principles**:

**1. Right person, right information, right time.**
Every role in QCI sees only what's relevant to them. An org admin sees everything. A project member sees their tasks. An executive sees financial health. Nobody drowns in irrelevant data.

**2. Every decision has a trail.**
Every approval, every budget change, every milestone update — it's logged, timestamped, and attributed. You always know who did what and when.

**3. Work where you are — no installation needed.**
Q-Flow runs in any browser. Mobile, desktop, laptop. Nothing to install. Always up to date.

The platform has **9 user roles**, organized in a hierarchy that mirrors how QCI actually works:

| Role | Who it represents | What they do |
|------|-------------------|--------------|
| Org Admin | CEO / DG / Director General | Full platform control |
| Division Admin | Head of Division | Creates verticals, onboards members, manages division config |
| **HoD** | **Head of Department** | **Approves/rejects requests, views MIS — no creation powers** |
| Vertical Head | Head of a vertical / practice | Manages vertical, updates lifecycle |
| Project Manager | PM responsible for a project | Owns projects, milestones, tasks |
| Team Lead | Senior member leading a sub-team | Manages sub-team tasks |
| Member | Individual contributor | Works on tasks, logs time |
| Executive | Board / senior leadership | Financial and portfolio view only |
| Viewer | External auditor / client | Read-only access |

*[Point to HoD in the table]*

One thing I want to call out specially — the **HoD role** is a deliberate design decision. In many organizations, the Head of Department is the approval authority but is not responsible for the day-to-day setup of teams and projects. We've separated those two things. The Division Admin creates, manages, and configures. The HoD approves, oversees, and sees the MIS. That's how QCI actually works.

Let me now walk through the platform **from each person's perspective.**"

### 5B — MILESTONE APPROVAL AND CLOSURE (3 minutes)

*[Navigate to a milestone with approval workflow]*

"Projects have **Milestones** — and milestones have gates.

When a PM believes a milestone is complete, they submit it for **Milestone Closure Approval.** This routes to the HoD, Division Admin, or Vertical Head depending on configuration.

The approver sees the full context — tasks completed, budget spent, effort logged — before deciding.

The milestone is closed only when approved. You can always prove that a milestone was formally signed off, who signed it, and when.

This is critical for client billing, compliance audits, and internal accountability.

And when a milestone is approved and closed, the **next milestone automatically unblocks** — no manual intervention needed."

---

### 5C — WATERFALL SEQUENCING (2 minutes)

*[Navigate to milestones with sequence numbers]*

"For programs that run in **waterfall** — where Milestone 2 cannot start until Milestone 1 is complete — Q-Flow enforces this automatically.

If Milestone 1 is incomplete, Milestone 2 shows as **BLOCKED** with a clear reason. Any attempt to progress it is rejected.

And this enforcement goes all the way down to tasks on the Kanban board — if Task B depends on Task A, and Task A is not done, the system won't let Task B be moved to Done. No workarounds."

## SECTION 7 — SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE (3 minutes)

"I know some of you have questions about data and security. Let me address that directly.

**Data residency:** Q-Flow's database is hosted on Supabase's AWS infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific region. Your data stays in Asia.

**Access control:** Nobody sees data they're not authorized to see. A team member cannot see another division's financial data. An external auditor gets a read-only view with no ability to modify anything. The HoD can see MIS and approvals for their department — nothing else.

**Audit trail:** Every action — create, edit, delete, approve — is logged with user ID, timestamp, and IP address. This is permanent and cannot be deleted by any user, including org admins.

**Budget integrity:** Milestone budgets are locked on creation. No one can quietly revise a budget after approval without going through the change process. The system enforces this automatically.

**Password security:** Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. We store no plain-text passwords.

**API security:** All communication is encrypted (HTTPS). Internal APIs use JWT tokens with expiry.

This platform meets standard enterprise security requirements and can be enhanced with SSO integration if required."

## SECTION 10 — Q&A PREPARATION (15 minutes)

*This section is for you to prepare, not to read aloud. Memorize these answers.*

---

### Q: "What if someone doesn't use the system? We've tried tools before."
**A:** "Great question — and an honest one. Tools fail for two reasons: they're too complex, or they're not enforced from the top. Q-Flow addresses the first by giving every user only what's relevant to their role — a member sees just their tasks. On enforcement, this is a leadership decision. But Q-Flow makes it easy — approvals can only happen in the system. If the HoD needs to approve something, it has to go through Q-Flow. That's enforcement by design."

---

### Q: "What's the difference between an HoD and a Division Admin?"
**A:** "Great question — this is intentional. The Division Admin is the builder: they create Verticals, onboard members, configure workflows, manage the division setup. The HoD is the authority: they approve or reject requests, see the MIS, and have financial oversight — but they don't touch the structural setup. In QCI's actual working model, these are different people with different responsibilities. We've reflected that exactly."

---

### Q: "How long does it take to train someone to use this?"
**A:** "For a team member, 15–20 minutes is enough — see tasks, log time, comment. For a project manager, 1–2 hours. For HoDs, about an hour focused on the Approval Inbox and MIS. For admins setting up divisions and workflows, half a day. We'll provide role-specific training sessions."

---

### Q: "Can it integrate with our existing tools — like Outlook, Excel, SAP?"
**A:** "Q-Flow has a built-in Outlook integration for calendar sync and task reminders. Every report is exportable to Excel. For ERP/SAP integration, there's a defined connection point in the Financial Dashboard — which can be wired as a Phase 2 item. It's not a limitation, it's a planned step."

---

### Q: "What happens to our existing project data?"
**A:** "We can migrate existing project structures — divisions, verticals, projects, milestones — via a bulk import. Historical task data would be added as a summary milestone, which keeps the system clean while preserving the record."

---

### Q: "What if the internet goes down?"
**A:** "Q-Flow requires internet connectivity — it's a web application. For offline scenarios, the platform's last-known state is cached in the browser for viewing. We recommend the export feature for offline backup of critical reports."

---

### Q: "Is this built only for QCI or can we white-label it?"
**A:** "The platform is built for QCI's exact organizational structure. The branding can be customized. The 9-role hierarchy and division structure are configurable for other government or enterprise organizations. That's a business decision, not a technical limitation."

---

### Q: "How many people can use this simultaneously?"
**A:** "The current deployment on Vercel can handle hundreds of concurrent users. For QCI's scale, it's more than sufficient. For thousands of users, we'd scale infrastructure accordingly."

---

### Q: "Who owns the data? What if the vendor goes away?"
**A:** "The data belongs entirely to QCI. It's in your organization's database instance. You can export all data at any time in standard formats. The codebase is on QCI's GitHub repository — you own the source code. There is no vendor lock-in."

---

### Q: "Can we assign tasks to external consultants or vendors?"
**A:** "Yes. Q-Flow has an External Users feature for this. Invite external consultants with restricted access — they see only the projects and tasks assigned to them. No access to financial data or other divisions."

---

### Q: "What's the cost?"
**A:** "Current infrastructure cost for hosting is approximately ₹X per month [fill in actual Vercel/Supabase cost]. That covers unlimited users within QCI. The development investment is already made — this is built and running. Going forward, cost is primarily maintenance and enhancement."

---

### Q: "Can the org chart / hierarchy be changed later?"
**A:** "Yes. Divisions can be created, renamed, or restructured. Verticals can be moved between divisions. Users can have their roles updated — including assigning someone as HoD who was previously a Division Admin. The system reflects your actual organization, which evolves."

---

### Q: "What about mobile access?"
**A:** "Q-Flow is fully responsive — it works on mobile browsers. Every page scales to a phone screen. A dedicated mobile app would be a Phase 3 enhancement if the demand is there."

---

### Q: "Can we see who is overloaded and who has capacity?"
**A:** "Yes — that's the Capacity Planning module. It shows hours allocated vs hours available per person, per week. Project Managers use this to make resourcing decisions before problems arise."

---

### Q: "How do approvals work if my approver is on leave?"
**A:** "The approval system supports multiple approvers in a group — any one of them can act. If you're using single-approver workflows, there's a delegation mechanism where the Org Admin can reassign pending approvals. This is a standard workflow configuration."

---

### Q: "Can a budget ever be changed once it's locked?"
**A:** "The planned budget of a milestone is locked on creation — this protects the approved budget from quiet revisions. The actual budget remains editable as real costs come in. If the planned budget genuinely needs to change, it goes through a formal change process with full audit trail. This is by design — it mirrors how proper financial governance works."
